## Typical Services to Ask For (Full Detail)

When contacting these businesses, a "full detail" or "complete detail" typically includes:

**Exterior:**
- Hand wash and dry
- Clay bar treatment (removes contaminants)
- Machine or hand polish/compound (paint correction)
- Carnauba or synthetic wax/sealant application
- Tire shine and wheel cleaning
- Window cleaning (exterior)
- Door jamb cleaning

**Interior:**
- Full vacuum (seats, carpets, trunk)
- Carpet and upholstery shampoo (hot water extraction)
- Leather cleaning and conditioning (if applicable)
- Dashboard, console, and hard surface cleaning with UV protectant
- Interior window cleaning
- Air vent cleaning
- Odor elimination/deodorizing
